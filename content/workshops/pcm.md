# Wednesday July 29th at 7:00PM

<br>

The summer heat has really kicked off this year and I'm sure you're looking for ways to stay cool. That's why this week, we are going to make some at-home cooling gel "Phase Change Material" or PCM for short. This incredible material hold a constant temperature of around 18°C for hours at a time! This may not seem very cold but trust us when we say that it will keep you cool through this crazy heatwave.

We will be following a recipe from Nighthawkinlight's video (found here) about PCMs and making it in-house with members. This is a really simple project you can do at home and it can really help in this blistering heat.
