## We were glad you came!

Holy Cow. What a day. With Mitch coming all the way from Vancouver to be here for our first official meet and greet, we had an absolute blast meeting our (admittedly small) community! We look forward to meeting more of you in the future!

## What was it all about?

We decided to host this event to share some of our plans for the future, what tools our members are looking for, and ways we can improve membership turnout. We also want this to become a reoccurring event where members and potential members can come and work on projects together. If we can start small, save up, and work our way into a larger building, we will be able to house all the huge project tools and art spaces members are looking for in no time!

We wanted to show off a few items we think community members may not have had a chance to get acquainted with and that work in a small workspace. With our plan to start small, we need to get more people on board so we can begin offering more comprehensive tools and work spaces in the near future.

## Plans going forward

We are starting to host bi-weekly get-togethers that encourage members to bring projects that they can either work on alone or with fellow like-minded members. I (Braeden) will be organizing these and working on my own projects at CanScribe College, 1226 Ellis Street, in a clean and quiet boardroom.

We'll keep updating our Substack to let everyone know when we will be opening the doors for collaboration, so subscribe to stay tuned in!
