# On Wednesday April 8th at 7:00PM...

<br>

We will be hosting a Micromouse building session!

Like our previous events, we will be working on our micromouse project. This time however, we will be tackling the electronic component section! We will be going over our parts and how they interact with each other, as well as starting on the basic layout of the device itself.

Please join us Wednesday, April 8th, 7:00PM, at 1226 Ellis Street for an opportunity to learn a new way of interacting with our world!

We will also be providing demo microcontrollers that will show off garden automation as well as a workstation to create your own systems.

As always, you can leave suggestions for future topics by filling out [this form](https://forms.gle/iUjkG2PtVFGyWuRV8), or by messaging us on our [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/create.community.society/). We look forward to seeing you there!
