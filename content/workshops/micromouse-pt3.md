# On Wednesday June 10th at 7:00PM...

<br>

Like our previous events, we will be working on our micro mouse project. This time however, we will be tackling the electronic component section! We will be going over our parts and how they interact with each other and integrating them with the new 3d design we made in our last two events.

Please join us Wednesday, June 10th , 7:00PM, at 1226 Ellis Street for an opportunity to learn a new way of interacting with our world!

We will be providing demo microcontrollers showing off garden automation and a workstation to try creating your own systems!

As always, you can leave suggestions for future topics by filling out [this form](https://forms.gle/iUjkG2PtVFGyWuRV8), or by messaging us on our [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/create.community.society/). We look forward to seeing you there!
