# On Wednesday May 13th at 7:00PM...

<br>

Come down and join us as we learn the ropes of 3D CAD! We are new to the 3D design process ourselves, so we will take our limited knowledge of the subject and try to design new functional models for other projects. We will be using [Onshape](https://www.onshape.com/en/) for our modelling as they offer mobile and desktop cloud design. If you have any experience, please come and share!

Please join us Wednesday, May 13th, 7:00PM, at 1226 Ellis Street for an opportunity to learn a new way of interacting with our world!

We will be providing demo designs and space for you to create your own models.

As always, you can leave suggestions for future topics by filling out [this form](https://forms.gle/iUjkG2PtVFGyWuRV8), or by messaging us on our [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/create.community.society/). We look forward to seeing you there!
