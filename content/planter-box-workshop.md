We hosting an afternoon workshop where everyone in the neighbourhood can get together and build planter boxes. If there is anyone who is unfamiliar with building and woodworking, then we will be able to offer instruction and guide them through the process. If someone does not want to build, but is still interested in having a planter box, we will try to have some available for community members to take home for free.

To make this event possible, we rely on donations to coverthe cost of materials. If you are interested in building planter boxes with us, see our link below to make a one-time donation (min. $20). With this dontation, you are guaranteed everything you need to build your own planter box!

If you are able to donate more than the minimum, we thank you immensely, as this helps us reach our goal and make it possble more people to join us.

If you are unable to donate, don't feel bad, you can still show up and hang out. And we may have some extra boxes leftover for you to take home.
