We are a not-for-profit community-shared workshop designed for individuals or groups to work on crafting, art, or skilled trade projects. We look to provide a third space for casual or professional use. We are accessible to anyone and will make strong efforts to provide financial support to those who would otherwise not be able to afford it. All profits generated contribute to a community fund intended for opening smaller spaces in other neighbourhoods, as well as lowering entry costs for current spaces. We take the initiative to reduce waste, encourage reuse, and reclaim old or unused materials. We utilize an in-house developed software for inventory management and space reservation that will provide real-time usage information of the space so members can plan their sessions however they would like.
